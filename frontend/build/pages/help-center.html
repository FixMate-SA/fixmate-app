Customer Support

How do I create a job request?

Creating a job request is simple:

  1. Click "Create New Job" on your dashboard
  2. Describe your repair needs in detail
  3. Upload photos if possible (helps fixers understand the job)
  4. Set your location and preferred timing
  5. Accept our terms and conditions
  6. Submit your request

Our AI will automatically match you with qualified fixers in your area!

How does the smart matching system work?

Our AI-powered matching considers multiple factors:

  • Proximity: Fixers closest to your location
  • Skills Match: Fixers with relevant expertise
  • Availability: Fixers currently available for work
  • Rating: Highly-rated fixers (≥3.0 stars or new fixers)
  • Payment Status: Fixers with no outstanding fees
  • Fair Distribution: Ensures equal opportunities for all fixers

Qualified fixers receive notifications and can accept your job first-come-first-served.

What happens if my fixer doesn't show up?

We have a comprehensive timeout system:

  • 180-Minute Window: Fixers have 3 hours to arrive after accepting
  • Auto-Escalation: If they don't arrive, your job is flagged as "EMERGENCY"
  • Automatic Reassignment: We immediately reassign your job to other qualified fixers
  • Fixer Penalty: The original fixer gets marked "unavailable" for 4 hours
  • No Cost to You: You don't pay anything if a fixer doesn't show up

Our system ensures you get reliable service or we find you someone who will!

How do payments work?

FixMate-SA uses a secure payment system:

  • R20 Platform Fee: Automatically charged per completed job
  • Direct Payment: Pay the fixer directly for their service
  • Payment Protection: Disputes hold payments until resolved
  • No Upfront Costs: Only pay after satisfactory completion
  • Multiple Methods: Cash, bank transfer, mobile money accepted

The R20 fee covers our safety features, support, and platform maintenance.

Can I cancel a job after it's been accepted?

Yes, you can cancel jobs with our flexible cancellation policy:

  • Before Fixer Arrival: Free cancellation anytime
  • Immediate Release: Job is immediately released for other fixers
  • No Penalties: No fees charged for customer cancellations
  • Notifications: Fixer receives automatic cancellation notice
  • Rebook Anytime: You can create a new job request immediately

Just use the "Cancel Job" button in your job details or dashboard.

What is photo verification and why is it important?

Photo verification ensures quality and protects you:

  • Before Photos: Fixers document the initial condition
  • Progress Photos: Track work as it happens
  • After Photos: Proof of completed work
  • AI Analysis: Our system checks photo quality automatically
  • Dispute Evidence: Photos serve as evidence if issues arise
  • Quality Assurance: Ensures work meets standards

This system has reduced disputes by 85% and improved job satisfaction significantly.

Fixer Support

How do I increase my chances of getting jobs?

Our smart matching prioritizes fixers who:

  • Complete Profile: Add photos, skills, and detailed descriptions
  • High Rating: Maintain a rating of 3.0 or above
  • Quick Response: Accept jobs quickly (first-come-first-served)
  • Stay Available: Keep your availability status updated
  • Pay Fees Promptly: Outstanding R20 fees block new jobs
  • Good Performance: Complete jobs on time with quality work
  • Fair Distribution: System ensures everyone gets opportunities

Consistent performance leads to more job matches!

What happens if I can't complete a job?

If you need to cancel a job:

  • Early Cancellation: Cancel ASAP to minimize impact
  • 2-Hour Freeze: You'll be marked unavailable for 2 hours
  • Rating Penalty: 0.2 rating point deduction
  • Customer Notification: Customer gets immediate notification
  • Job Reassignment: Job is reassigned to other qualified fixers

Important: Repeated cancellations (>25%) can affect your matching priority. Communicate honestly with customers and only accept jobs you can complete.

How does the R20 platform fee work?

The R20 fee covers platform services:

  • When Charged: After each completed job
  • Payment Deadline: Must be paid within 48 hours
  • What It Covers: Safety features, support, matching system, dispute resolution
  • Outstanding Fees: Block you from receiving new jobs
  • Suspension: Unpaid fees lead to 48-hour suspension
  • Payment Methods: Bank transfer, mobile money, in-app payment

Keep your fees current to maintain access to job opportunities!

Why do I need to take photos?

Photos protect both you and the customer:

  • Before Photos: Document initial conditions to avoid blame
  • Progress Photos: Show your professional work process
  • After Photos: Prove job completion and quality
  • Dispute Protection: Photos serve as evidence in disputes
  • Quality Assurance: Demonstrates your professional standards
  • Customer Confidence: Customers see your work quality

Good photo documentation has helped resolve 95% of disputes in fixers' favor!

How do I handle difficult customers or disputes?

We have a comprehensive support system:

  • Stay Professional: Always maintain professional communication
  • Document Everything: Take photos and keep records
  • Use Dispute System: File disputes through the app
  • Admin Mediation: Our team mediates fairly
  • Payment Protection: Payments are held during disputes
  • Evidence-Based: Decisions based on photos and facts
  • Emergency Support: 24/7 support for urgent issues

Remember: Your safety is our priority. Use the emergency button if you feel unsafe.
