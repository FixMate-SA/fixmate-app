Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to join FixMate-SA?

Joining FixMate-SA is completely free! We only charge a small R20 platform fee per completed job, which covers our payment processing, safety features, and support services.

How do I receive job notifications?

You'll receive job notifications through the FixMate-SA app, SMS, or WhatsApp - whatever works best for you. Our smart matching system only sends you relevant jobs based on your skills and location.

What if I can't complete a job?

Life happens! You can cancel jobs with minimal penalties if done early. However, repeated cancellations may affect your matching priority. We encourage honest communication with customers.

How do I get paid?

Payments are processed automatically after job completion and customer approval. The R20 platform fee is deducted, and you receive the rest. Payment methods include bank transfer, mobile money, and cash.

Do I need my own tools and transport?

Yes, fixers are expected to have their own tools and reliable transportation to job sites. This ensures you can provide professional service and maintain your independence as a contractor.
