FixMate-SA is a South African mobile/web application designed to connect clients with service professionals through a FixMate Job Request and Assignment Workflow. Key features include user authentication (Admin, Fixer, Client roles), job management, fixer profiles, ratings, and multi-channel support (SMS/USSD, WhatsApp). The overarching goal is a comprehensive platform with refined role-based routing, improved UI/UX, and Afrikaans localization.

User data isolation has been a priority, ensuring privacy across modules. Most recently, an automatic job allocation system has been implemented to assign jobs to fixers and send notifications, with associated frontend integrations for fixers to view available jobs and notifications.
