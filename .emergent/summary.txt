It encompasses user authentication (Admin, Fixer, Client), job management, fixer profiles, ratings, and multi-channel support (SMS/USSD, WhatsApp). The objective is to be a comprehensive platform with refined role-based routing, improved UI/UX, and Afrikaans localization.
